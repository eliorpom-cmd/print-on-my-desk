The bridge

This tab is your printer's connection to the internet. While it is open, messages come out. When you close it, they wait.

1 · The printer

Turn the printer on first. Your browser will ask which device to use — it is the one called MXW01.

2 · The queue

The token is kept in this browser and lets whoever has it take messages out of your queue. Fine on your own computer; not fine on a shared one. Use “Forget the token” when you are done.
